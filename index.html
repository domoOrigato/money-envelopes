Bring your numbers over

On your computer, open Money Envelopes, go to Add statements and press Save backup file. Get that file onto this phone (email it to yourself, or save it to Google Drive).

Then come back here, open Add statements, and tap Restore from backup.

Add statements

Download your transactions as CSV files from RBC or Capital One online banking, then choose them here. Your phone warns you if an envelope passes its alert level. Anything already added is skipped, so it's safe to drop the same file twice.

Backup

Your data lives only on this phone. If the phone is lost or the app is removed, it's gone, so save a backup now and then and keep it somewhere safe.

Settings

Envelope colour

Tap a knob to pick a colour, or colour each envelope by how full it is (green, then amber, then red when it runs over).

Desktop wallpaper

Turn these envelopes into your Windows wallpaper. Anyone who can see your screen will see it, so you can hide the dollar amounts.

Once it's on, it repaints itself every 6 hours (4 times a day), even when the app is closed. "Put my old wallpaper back" switches that off.
